# AC-INF traceability rows

10 acceptance-criterion rows with the `AC-INF-` prefix, in their original matrix order. See [README.md](./README.md) for the matrix purpose, maintenance rules, and the full prefix index.

[ADR 0037](../../adr/0037-single-host-production-without-hosted-uat.md) replaced the fleet, CloudFront, and hosted-UAT target of [ADR 0034](../../adr/0034-scheduled-uat-and-production-autoscaling.md) with the [single-host design](../../design/single-host-production.md). Rows 001, 002, and 004 to 006 are restated for single-host production. Rows 003 and 007 to 010 are `DEFERRED` because their target no longer exists. No row is proven by configuration, `tofu validate`, a healthy service, or public health checks alone. The native ARM64 image build adds runtime smoke and provenance evidence without adding a row.
