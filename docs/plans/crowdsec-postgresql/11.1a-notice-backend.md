## Required implementation

- Add the two durable tables and explicit `aboutme_app` grants exactly as specified. The fixed campaign is `crowdsec-postgres-paths-v1`; the ledger contains no address, name, token, message body, provider identifier, or SMTP reply.
- Capture the quiescent `users` cohort with one fixed cutoff and UUID cursor in transactions of at most 100 rows. Seal only after count equality and a bidirectional membership proof. Resume the same cutoff and cursor after interruption.
- Compile one manifest digest over the exact bilingual public copy, fixed bilingual email copy, canonical `https://aboutme.vn/privacy` link, and publication date `2026-10-19`. Every batch and transition must verify the campaign and digest.
- Add strict encrypted payload version 3 and the closed `privacy_notice` kind. The payload contains only `version`, `to`, `userId`, `noticeId`, and `generation`; reject duplicate or unknown fields and mismatched live state.
- Keep authentication and security mail ahead of notice mail. Keep the existing lease, retry, backoff, send timeout, claim batch, concurrency, authenticated encryption, key rotation, and seven-day outbox cleanup behavior.
- Before claim and again before SMTP, require current read-only host send authority and no restore marker. Ordinary mail must continue when notice authority is absent or invalid.
- Make outbox and ledger transitions atomic for accepted and terminal outcomes. Preserve one logical job ID through ordinary retry. Clear encrypted payloads as current policy requires without erasing lifetime ledger evidence.
- Make account deletion lock user, notice recipient, then outbox job; resolve a non-accepted cohort member as deleted in the same transaction; never lower the original cohort count or send after deletion commits.
- Expose only `prepare`, `enqueue`, `status`, and `activate` under `server crowdsec-privacy-notice`. Accept no recipient, message, URL, campaign, cutoff, or success-override arguments. Enforce the specified batch, backlog, statement, lock, and whole-command bounds.

Implement activation, abort, retirement, late-delivery-failure, restore-reconciliation, generation-recovery, receipt-validation, and recipient-purge rules exactly as accepted. Irrecoverable failure for an active account can only abort the campaign.
